SEBI Mutual Fund Categorization Guidelines and Riskometer FAQ.
SEBI guidelines specify the classification of mutual funds into 5 broad groups: Equity, Debt, Hybrid, Solution-Oriented, and Other schemes.
- Riskometer: The Riskometer must have six risk levels: Low, Low to Moderate, Moderate, Moderately High, High, and Very High. It must be evaluated monthly based on portfolio characteristics.
- ELSS: Equity Linked Savings Scheme must invest at least 80% in equities and carry a mandatory 3-year lock-in period.
