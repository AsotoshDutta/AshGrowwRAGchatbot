AMFI Exit Load Rules. An exit load is a fee charged to investors when they redeem or switch out of a mutual fund scheme within a specified timeframe. - Purpose: To discourage short-term trading and protect long-term investors. - Calculation: Calculated as a percentage of the Net Asset Value (NAV) at redemption. For SBI equity funds, the standard exit load is 1% if redeemed within 1 year, and Nil thereafter. Last updated from sources: May 2026.
