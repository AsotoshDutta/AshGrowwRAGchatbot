SBI Mutual Fund Statement Download and Service Help. To download your SBI Mutual Fund Account Statement or Capital Gains Report, follow these steps: Step 1: Visit the official SBI Mutual Fund portal (https://www.sbimf.com) and navigate to the 'Quick Links' section. Step 2: Click on 'Statement of Account' or 'Capital Gains Statement'. Step 3: Enter your registered Email ID, PAN number, or Folio Number. Step 4: Enter the OTP sent to your registered mobile number or email address. Step 5: Select the financial year or custom date range and download the statement PDF. For tax purposes, capital gains reports are last updated up to the current assessment year.
