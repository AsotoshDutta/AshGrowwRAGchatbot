AMFI Total Expense Ratio (TER) Rules. The Total Expense Ratio (TER) represents the annual operating expenses of a mutual fund scheme, expressed as a percentage of the fund's daily net assets. - Capped limits: Under SEBI Regulation 52, the maximum TER limit for equity schemes is 2.25% for the first ₹500 Crores of AUM, scaling down as AUM increases. - Regular vs Direct: Direct plans have a lower expense ratio since they do not include advisor commission or distribution costs.
