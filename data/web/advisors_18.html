SEBI Registered Investment Advisors (RIA) Information. Under SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013, no person can provide investment advice unless registered with SEBI. - Disclaimer: Factual queries about scheme features do not constitute investment advice. - Directory: To get personalized investment advice, consult a SEBI Registered Investment Advisor.
