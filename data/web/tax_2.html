SBI Mutual Fund Capital Gains Taxation Guide. Taxation rules for SBI Mutual Fund schemes depend on the asset class: 1. Equity Mutual Funds (e.g., SBI Bluechip, SBI Small Cap, SBI Contra, SBI Focused Equity, SBI Long Term Equity): - Short-term Capital Gains (STCG): If units are redeemed within 1 year, gains are taxed at 15%. - Long-term Capital Gains (LTCG): If units are redeemed after 1 year, gains exceeding ₹1 Lakh in a financial year are taxed at 10% without indexation. 2. Debt Mutual Funds (e.g., SBI Magnum Constant Maturity Fund): - For investments made on or after April 1, 2023, all capital gains are treated as short-term and taxed at the investor's applicable income tax slab rate.
