SBI Long Term Equity Fund (ELSS) Scheme Details. SBI Long Term Equity Fund is an open-ended Equity Linked Saving Scheme (ELSS) offering tax benefits under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act. - Fund Manager: Dinesh Balachandran. He has been managing this fund since September 2016. He has over 7 years of tenure on this fund. - Lock-in Period: Mandatory 3-year lock-in period from the date of unit allotment. No withdrawals are permitted before the completion of 3 years. - Exit Load: Nil exit load applies to units after the completion of the 3-year lock-in period. - Expense Ratio: The Total Expense Ratio (TER) is 1.64% for the Regular Plan and 0.95% for the Direct Plan. - Minimum SIP Amount: ₹500 and in multiples of ₹500. - Riskometer Classification: Very High Risk. - Benchmark Index: S&P BSE 500 TRI.
