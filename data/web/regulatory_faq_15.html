AMFI Mutual Fund Category Explanations. Association of Mutual Funds in India (AMFI) Knowledge Center: - Equity Schemes: Invest primarily in shares/stocks. Capped categories include Large Cap (top 100 stocks), Mid Cap (101-250th stocks), and Small Cap (251st and below). - Debt Schemes: Invest in fixed income securities (treasury bills, government bonds, commercial paper). - ELSS: A category of equity funds offering tax saving under Sec 80C with a 3-year lock-in.
